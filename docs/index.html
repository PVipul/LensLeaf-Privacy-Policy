# Privacy Policy **Last Updated: September 6, 2026** LensLeaf ("LensLeaf", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how LensLeaf collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the LensLeaf mobile application (the "App"). By using LensLeaf, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. --- ## 1. About LensLeaf LensLeaf is a plant identification application that allows users to identify plants using images captured with the device camera or selected from the device gallery. The App may also allow users to create an account, save plant identification results, view scan history, manage their profile, and delete their account. --- ## 2. Information We Collect Depending on how you use LensLeaf, we may collect the following information. ### 2.1 Account Information When you create an account using email/password authentication, we may collect: * Email address * Password authentication information * Display name or name provided by you Passwords are handled through Firebase Authentication. LensLeaf does not directly store your password in its own database. If you sign in using Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as: * Name * Email address * Profile picture Authentication is handled using Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In. --- ### 2.2 Plant Images When you use the plant identification feature, LensLeaf may access an image that you: * Capture using your device camera; or * Select from your device gallery. The image is used to provide the plant identification functionality. Depending on the feature and implementation, the selected image may be transmitted to the plant identification service used by LensLeaf for analysis. --- ### 2.3 Plant Identification Information When you identify a plant, LensLeaf may process and store information associated with the identification result, including: * Plant name * Scientific name * Family name * Identification confidence score * Identification result information * Date and time of the scan * Plant image or image URL * Additional identification response data returned by the identification service This information may be associated with your account so that you can view your previous scans and saved plant information. --- ### 2.4 Profile Information If you create an account, LensLeaf may store information such as: * Display name * Email address * Profile picture, when provided by an authentication provider --- ### 2.5 Device Permissions LensLeaf may request access to: **Camera:** Used to capture plant images for identification. **Photos/Gallery:** Used to allow you to select a plant image from your device for identification. LensLeaf does not access your camera or photos continuously in the background. Permissions are used only when required for the corresponding feature. --- ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information collected through LensLeaf for the following purposes: * To create and manage your LensLeaf account * To authenticate users * To provide plant identification functionality * To analyze plant images * To display plant identification results * To save and display your scan history * To store saved plant information * To maintain and improve the functionality of the App * To provide account management features * To process account deletion requests * To protect the security and integrity of the App * To respond to support or privacy-related requests We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to the functionality of LensLeaf unless disclosed to you or required by applicable law. --- ## 4. Third-Party Services LensLeaf uses certain third-party services to provide its functionality. ### 4.1 Firebase LensLeaf uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Storage. Firebase may process information necessary to provide: * User authentication * Account management * Storage of application data * Storage of plant images * Storage of scan history Firebase data is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices. For more information, please refer to Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy --- ### 4.2 Google Sign-In LensLeaf may allow users to sign in using Google Sign-In. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide LensLeaf with information such as your name, email address, and profile picture, depending on the information available through your Google account and the permissions granted. Google Sign-In is optional if other supported authentication methods are available. For more information: https://policies.google.com/privacy --- ### 4.3 Plant Identification Service LensLeaf uses a third-party plant identification service to analyze plant images and return identification results. When you request plant identification, the selected plant image and/or information necessary to process the request may be transmitted to the plant identification service. The service may process the image to identify the plant and return information such as: * Plant name * Scientific name * Family * Confidence score * Other identification information LensLeaf uses the service only to provide the plant identification functionality. --- ## 5.
