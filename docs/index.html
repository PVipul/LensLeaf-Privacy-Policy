01About LensLeaf

LensLeaf is a plant identification application that allows users to identify plants using images captured with the device camera or selected from the device gallery.

The App may also allow users to create an account, save plant identification results, view scan history, manage their profile, and delete their account.

02Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LensLeaf, we may collect the following information.

Account Information

When you create an account using email/password authentication, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Password authentication information
  • Display name or name provided by you

Passwords are handled through Firebase Authentication. LensLeaf does not directly store your password in its own database.

If you sign in using Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture

Authentication is handled using Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.

Plant Images

When you use the plant identification feature, LensLeaf may access an image that you:

  • Capture using your device camera; or
  • Select from your device gallery.

The image is used to provide the plant identification functionality. Depending on the feature and implementation, the selected image may be transmitted to the plant identification service used by LensLeaf for analysis.

Plant Identification Information

When you identify a plant, LensLeaf may process and store information associated with the identification result, including:

  • Plant name
  • Scientific name
  • Family name
  • Identification confidence score
  • Identification result information
  • Date and time of the scan
  • Plant image or image URL
  • Additional identification response data returned by the identification service

This information may be associated with your account so that you can view your previous scans and saved plant information.

Profile Information

If you create an account, LensLeaf may store information such as:

  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture, when provided by an authentication provider

Device Permissions

Camera: Used to capture plant images for identification.

Photos/Gallery: Used to allow you to select a plant image from your device for identification.

LensLeaf does not access your camera or photos continuously in the background. Permissions are used only when required for the corresponding feature.

03How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through LensLeaf for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your LensLeaf account
  • To authenticate users
  • To provide plant identification functionality
  • To analyze plant images
  • To display plant identification results
  • To save and display your scan history
  • To store saved plant information
  • To maintain and improve the functionality of the App
  • To provide account management features
  • To process account deletion requests
  • To protect the security and integrity of the App
  • To respond to support or privacy-related requests

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to the functionality of LensLeaf unless disclosed to you or required by applicable law.

04Third-Party Services

LensLeaf uses certain third-party services to provide its functionality.

Firebase

LensLeaf uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Storage. Firebase may process information necessary to provide user authentication, account management, storage of application data, storage of plant images, and storage of scan history.

Firebase data is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices. Google privacy information.

Google Sign-In

LensLeaf may allow users to sign in using Google Sign-In. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide LensLeaf with information such as your name, email address, and profile picture, depending on the information available through your Google account and the permissions granted. Google Sign-In is optional if other supported authentication methods are available.

More information about Google's privacy practices.

Plant Identification Service

LensLeaf uses a third-party plant identification service to analyze plant images and return identification results. When you request plant identification, the selected plant image and/or information necessary to process the request may be transmitted to the plant identification service.

The service may process the image to identify the plant and return information such as plant name, scientific name, family, confidence score, and other identification information. LensLeaf uses the service only to provide the plant identification functionality.

05Data Sharing

LensLeaf does not sell your personal information.

We may share or transmit information with third-party service providers when necessary to provide App functionality. These providers may include:

  • Firebase / Google services for authentication, cloud storage, and database functionality
  • Google Sign-In for authentication when you choose to use it
  • Plant identification services for analyzing plant images

We do not share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes unless this is clearly disclosed to you and permitted by applicable law.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, governmental request, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of LensLeaf, its users, or others.

06Storage, Retention & Deletion

Data Storage

Certain information may be stored on secure third-party infrastructure used by LensLeaf. Depending on the features you use, this may include account information, plant scan information, plant identification results, plant images, saved plant information, and scan timestamps.

LensLeaf uses Firebase services for certain cloud-based data storage and authentication functionality. Firebase provides security mechanisms for protecting data, while access to stored data is controlled through application security rules and authentication mechanisms.

Local Data Storage

LensLeaf may store certain application information locally on your device to provide features such as scan history or offline access to previously saved information. Local information may include scan information, plant identification results, local image references, timestamps, and other information required for the application's local functionality.

Deleting the App may remove locally stored application data depending on your device's operating system and storage behavior.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services and features of LensLeaf, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain legitimate business or security purposes.

Account-related data may be retained while your account remains active. When you request account deletion, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify account-associated information under our control, subject to technical limitations and any information that we are legally required to retain. Third-party service providers may have their own retention practices.

Account Deletion

LensLeaf provides users with an option to delete their account from within the App. When you request account deletion, the account associated with your LensLeaf authentication profile will be deleted.

We also intend to delete account-associated application data under our control, including applicable profile information, cloud-stored scan information, saved plant information, and stored plant images associated with the account. Some information may need to be retained when required by law, necessary for security purposes, or otherwise permitted by applicable regulations.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open LensLeaf.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.
  5. Complete the authentication confirmation when requested.

Account deletion is permanent and may not be reversible.

07Security & Children's Privacy

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through LensLeaf. These measures may include Firebase Authentication, authenticated access to user data, Firebase security rules, secure communication using HTTPS/TLS where supported, access controls, and limiting access to information to purposes necessary for the App.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

LensLeaf is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to LensLeaf without appropriate parental or guardian involvement, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

If applicable laws in your country define a different minimum age for processing personal information, those requirements may apply.

No Sale of Personal Information

LensLeaf does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers when necessary to provide App functionality, such as authentication, cloud storage, or plant identification.
